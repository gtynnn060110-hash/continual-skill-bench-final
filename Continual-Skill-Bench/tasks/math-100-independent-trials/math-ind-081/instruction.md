## Task 81 [L6] [realmath] [algebra]

Let $I$ be a monomial ideal in a polynomial ring $R$ of height $h$ with an irredundant reduced primary decomposition
\[
I = Q_1 \cap \cdots \cap Q_r \cap Q_{r+1} \cap \cdots \cap Q_t,
\]
where for $1 \leq i \leq r$, we have \(\height(Q_i)=h\), and for $r+1 \leq i \leq t$, \(\height(Q_i)>h\). For any integer \(s \geq 1\), what is the multiplicity \(\mult(R/I^s)\) expressed in terms of \(\mult(R/I)\), \(h\), \(s\), and the exponents \(a_{i_j}\)?
